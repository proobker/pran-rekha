## Full script (read-through)

App Demo
- Hospital le pahilai visit gareka patients haru lai register garxa.
- Lab le medical reports scan garxa, ra phone kai OCR le report line by line read garxa.
- Patient le responders haru le k-kasto information dekhna paune bhanera decide garna milxa.
- Real life ma one card tap le patient-approved medical summary paramedic ko device ma transfer hunxa.
- Yes bata patient hospital aaunu agadi nai responders haru le patient ko awastha thaha pauna sakxan.
- Card nabhako awastha ma local face matching bata pani patient identify garna milxa.
- Patient le aafno data kasle access gareko xa bhanera pani herna milxa.

Hardware Demo
- Hamro hardware ma RFID scanner, Raspberry Pi 3 ra LCD display xa.
- RFID scanner le card ma store gareko UID scan garxa.
- Raspberry Pi 3 ko through UID server ma confirmation ko lagi pathauxa.
- Patient ko identity confirm bhayepaxi LCD display ma information dekhauxa.

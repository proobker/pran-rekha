## Full script (read-through)

This is Pran Rekha. Let's walk through the demo.
A hospital registers the patient, a lab scans a report. On-device OCR, checked line by line. The patient decides what responders can see, and can revoke it anytime. In the field, one card tap brings the approved summary to the paramedic's phone, allergies first. Before the ambulance arrives, the ER already knows the ETA, blood group and allergies. No card? A local face match finds them. Every access is logged for the patient, even offline.
